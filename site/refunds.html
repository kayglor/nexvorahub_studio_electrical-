1. 14-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your first purchase of a NexvoraHub Studio Pro plan, ask for a refund within 14 days of the purchase date and we will refund the full amount, whether or not you have used the Pro features. This applies once per customer and per plan.

2. Cancelling a subscription

  • You can cancel at any time from the link in your purchase or renewal email (or by contacting us). Cancelling stops future renewals.
  • Your Pro features stay active until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Renewal payments are not refundable once the new period has started, except as set out in sections 3 and 4 or where the law requires.
  • If you forget to cancel before an annual renewal, contact us within 7 days of the renewal and, if you have not used Pro features since the renewal, we will refund it.

3. Faults and changes on our side

  • If a Pro feature you paid for does not work as described and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, we will refund the affected period.
  • If we withdraw a Pro feature that is a main part of your plan, or end your licence without cause, we will refund the unused part of your subscription.

4. Your statutory rights

If you buy as a consumer, you have legal rights that this policy does not reduce. In the UK and EU, digital services can normally be cancelled within 14 days; if you ask for access to start immediately, that right may end once the service has been fully provided. Our 14-day money-back guarantee applies in any case. Business purchases are covered by this policy and our Terms of service.

5. How refunds are paid

Licences are sold by our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy. Refunds go back to the original payment method, normally within 5–10 working days of approval, in the currency you paid (including any sales tax charged). When a refund is issued, the related licence key is deactivated.

6. How to ask for a refund

Email [support email] with your order number (from your receipt email) and, if you wish, the reason. We reply within 2 working days. Please contact us before opening a chargeback with your bank so we can resolve it quickly.

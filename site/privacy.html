4. Browser storage

The Service saves your current project (eld-project-v1) and, for Pro users, your licence (nvh-licence-v1) in your browser’s local storage so your work is still there when you return. This storage is used only because you use the Service, is not shared, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings or with “New blank project”. We do not use cookies.

5. Service providers and international transfers

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) – website hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • Google LLC (USA) – Google Fonts.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) – domain name system (DNS).
  • Lemon Squeezy, LLC (USA) – merchant of record for licence sales, payments, tax and licence keys. Lemon Squeezy acts as an independent controller for payment data under its own privacy policy.

These providers may process data outside the UK and European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. Where they do, transfers are protected by an adequacy decision (such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified) or by standard contractual clauses / the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

6. How long we keep data

  • Order and licence records: for the life of the licence plus 6 years, to meet tax and accounting law.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.
  • Hosting and font-delivery logs: kept by the providers for their standard periods.
  • Browser storage: until you clear it.
